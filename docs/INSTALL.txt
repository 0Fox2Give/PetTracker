PET TRACKER
Aion 2 pet collection tracker • Windows x64 • beta

GET STARTED
1. Download the installer from github.com/0Fox2Give/PetTracker/releases/tag/updates.
   Run PetTracker.Desktop-win-stable-Setup.exe to install Pet Tracker.
2. Install Npcap from https://npcap.com/#download if not already installed.
   Npcap is required for live tracking and is not bundled with Pet Tracker.
   Launch Pet Tracker from your desktop or Start menu before starting Aion 2.
   A normal launch shows the tracker even if you hid it in the previous session.
   If Only show over Aion 2 is enabled, bring the game to the foreground.
3. Your first detected character creates My account and imports its collection.
   No account selection or setup is needed. If the game was already logged in
   when tracking started, return to character selection and log in again once.

No website account or sign-in is required. The app runtime and game portraits
are included. If capture is unavailable, Pet Tracker opens connection help with
a link to Npcap's official installer. Npcap installation may need Windows
administrator approval. Follow its restart instructions, then restart Pet Tracker.

BLACK CLOUD MERCHANT
Open the merchant with Pet Tracker running and your collection synchronized.
The overlay lists recently seen chest/merchant pets that you have not maxed,
with their rank, soul progress, shop tab and Kina price. Pets with known mob sources are excluded.
No purchase is made for you. Confirm the stock in game before buying.
Offers hide after two minutes, at shop refresh, or when tracking loses continuity.
Settings > Tracking > Black Cloud Merchant controls the shopping list. It replaces the
desktop merchant notification; rank notification settings are separate.

PET TRACKER
Drag the header to move the tracker. Its gear opens Settings. Use the Windows
tray paw icon if the tracker is hidden, fixed or passing clicks to the game.
Double-click the tray icon to open Settings. Use Aion 2's Pets menu for the full
collection; this addon focuses on recent activity and your pinned farming targets.

Five recent pet types appear by default. Inactive rows fade after 20 seconds.
New soul rewards or supported participated kills renew that pet's row. Expiry
only hides activity; your collection remains saved. Hide maxed is optional.

Only received souls update points. Unranked uses /5, Rank 1 /25, Rank 2 /75 and
Rank 3 shows MAX. If stream continuity is lost, valid rewards still refresh the
recent list while point totals stay last-known until a fresh collection arrives. A promoted pet with an unknown current balance
shows ? rather than an invented total. Start tracking before logging into Aion 2
so the initial collection can be received.
The independent combat decoder covers limited verified forms; received soul
rewards are the primary source of pet activity.

NEARBY
Nearby lists pet mobs reported to your game client; it has no measured radius.
Sightings older than 15 seconds show a Last seen label. Settings > Tracking lets
you hide unrefreshed mobs after 15, 30, 60, 120 or 300 seconds. New installations
use 60 seconds; existing preferences are preserved. Stationary mobs may expire
without leaving the area. Game-reported removals clear sightings immediately.
Pinned pets remain visible and say Not currently observed when no sighting remains.
Observations never add souls or change saved progress.
With Unfinished first, Nearby uses name order within unfinished and maxed groups.
Routine sightings do not reshuffle the list. Recent only explicitly sorts newest
first; Closest to next rank follows progress with name order for ties. Pins stay first.

FIND A PET
Use the search icon in the tracker header or Find a pet in its tray menu. Search
by pet or mob name, then choose a region to open its farming map in your browser.
You can also right-click a recent pet and choose Find on map. Maps show known
spawn locations, not whether a mob is currently alive. Region access can vary.
Pets without a verified map link say so.

Pin this pet keeps a target at the top in Recent and Nearby until you unpin it.
Pin up to three pets by default. Settings > Tracking > Maximum pinned pets lets
you choose a limit from one to five. Pins stay in the order you added them.
Right-click a tracker row or use Find a pet to pin or unpin an individual pet.
Pins stay visible even with expiry or Hide maxed. The row limit grows if needed
to leave room for other activity. Lowering the pin limit preserves existing pins;
unpin some before adding more. Your old single pin is kept when upgrading.
Pins save automatically and never change soul totals.

BLACK MARKET
Find a pet includes a Black Market watchlist for pets without a confirmed
farmable mob source. Random-chest alternatives are included; maxed pets are hidden.
Start tracking before login, then open the shop's Special or Pets tab to read
offers. Seen in Black Market shows supported offers recently received from the
game, with their observed price. Opening another tab replaces the observation.
Check the shop before buying: offers clear after two minutes, their refresh time,
or a connection/character change. Unsupported stock layouts are not displayed.
The tracker cannot check unopened shops or notify about unseen future stock.

Settings > Tracking > Black Cloud Merchant controls the overlay shopping list and explains
whether capture, collection sync or fresh stock is missing. Recommendations need
a synchronized collection and verified progress for a non-max pet on the watchlist.
After a recommended pet receives souls, its row shows the updated total for 20
seconds, then leaves the shopping list. This works in Recent and Nearby. It follows
observed rewards, not an independently decoded purchase receipt. The tracker never
buys items, refreshes the shop, or changes your totals from an offer.

Collection sync is automatic at character login; there is no sync button. Leave
Pet Tracker running, return to character selection, then log back in. If totals
are still missing, check Settings > Help. The first unlock can still get stuck on
Rank increase pending; this remains under investigation. Relogging is a workaround.
Hover a pet row to see souls remaining when its current progress is known.

ACCOUNTS
Pets are account-wide. If you use one game account, enable Settings > Account >
Account > Share progress across all characters to connect new alts automatically
with your selected collection. Start the tracker before logging in. Turn sharing
off before using a different game account. Existing profiles are preserved and a
backup is made when enabling sharing.

For multiple game accounts, use Manage accounts to choose the correct collection
and link the detected character. Account assignment is local because no stable
game-account field has been verified. Do not create a separate collection for
every alt.

YOUR DATA
Pet progress and preferences save automatically. Updates keep
them separately from program files under %LocalAppData%\PetTracker. Background
backups and automatic recovery handle missing or damaged local data; restored
points remain last-known until the game confirms current values. No manual
collection import, export, backup or restore is needed. Share the clean installer
or portable ZIP with friends, not your private local data folder.

SETTINGS
Tracking: mode, row limit, pin limit, Hide maxed and the merchant shopping list.
Expand Sorting and timing for sort order and Recent/Nearby expiry controls.
Overlay: size, background opacity, game-only visibility and click behavior.
More overlay options contains the shortcut, Hide when empty and reduced motion.
Rank notifications contains desktop notification and sound preferences.
Account: shared-character progress, account management and collection sync help.
Windows startup and start-hidden preferences have been retired. The installed
app removes its recognized legacy startup entry on launch when Windows allows it.
Old cosmetic preferences are kept; Reset this section on Overlay restores the
standard appearance as well as its visible controls. Tracking reset preserves
accounts and pinned pets. Account settings have no reset action.
Updates: check and download releases, choose Stable or Beta, then restart to install.
The installed edition checks for updates automatically. Download and restart from
Settings > Updates; automatic downloading is optional. Your game stays open.
Older copies need this installer once to enable future updates. Quit the older
tracker through its tray menu before installing. Saved data stays on this PC.
Help: connection assessment and a support report you can copy or save. Reports
exclude character/account names, chat, packet samples and private file paths.
Nothing is uploaded automatically.

Display settings save automatically. Reset this section restores only that page's
preferences; it preserves pet progress, accounts, connection overrides and pins.
Hide when empty automatically hides an idle connected tracker. The tray Show
action or tracker shortcut can reveal it; new activity restores automatic hiding.
Connection overrides are under Help > Advanced troubleshooting > Connection
overrides. Refresh adapters lists available choices; Automatic is recommended.
Apply connection options saves these fields and restarts capture. Restarting may
require a new game collection; do not repeatedly relog before checking status.

Closing a window keeps capture running. Quit Pet Tracker in the tray menu stops
it. The portable ZIP must be extracted before running; it uses the same default
data location as the installed app. Run one copy for your chosen data location.

FINDER SOURCES
Search by pet or linked mob name. Unnamed/internal catalog records are hidden.
Shop and campaign pets show verified acquisition instructions and an official
source link where available. Unknown acquisition routes are labeled unverified.
Only currently supported regions appear. Map markers show known spawn locations,
not live proximity or guaranteed current availability.

NEARBY PET MOBS
Use Recent / Nearby below the tracker header, or Settings > Tracking > Tracker mode.
Recent keeps your existing reward list. Nearby lists supported pet mobs reported
by the game client before you attack. Several mobs sharing a pet use one row.
Hide maxed, row limit, sort, portraits, progress bars and your pinned targets work
in both modes. A pinned target remains visible even when it is not nearby.

Nearby does not measure distance, scan the map or reveal undiscovered mobs. Only
verified mob-to-pet links are shown. Soul totals and ranks still come from the
collection/reward tracker; seeing a mob never adds souls or kills.

Mobs leave the list when a removal/death is received. Capture interruptions,
character/connection changes and fresh local-player records clear sightings.
Help > Advanced troubleshooting > Nearby observation timeout supports 60, 120 or
300 seconds (default 120). A stationary mob can time out until the client reports
it again. The Recent expiry timer does not apply to Nearby. Start tracking before
entering an area so its appearance messages can be observed.

Walking direction arrows and distance readouts are not included in this release.
